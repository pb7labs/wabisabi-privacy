Privacy Policy

App: WabiSabi - Mood Tracker & Diary | Effective Date: March 30, 2025

🔒 100% Local & Offline Privacy Guarantee: Your personal journal entries, notes, photos, mood logs, and voice recordings are saved exclusively on your local device. We never upload, transfer, or store your personal diary entries on external servers.

Welcome to WabiSabi - Mood Tracker & Diary ("we," "our," or "us"), developed by Pb7 Labs. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this Privacy Policy. This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you use our mobile application ("Application").

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Personal Journal Data (Offline & Local Only)

All personal content created inside the Application—including diary titles, text entries, mood records, paper theme selections, custom stamps, attached photos, and voice memos—is stored exclusively on your device's internal storage.

  • No Cloud Sync: Personal diary data is never transmitted to, processed by, or stored on our servers or any third-party cloud database.
  • User Control: You have complete control over your content and can delete individual entries or clear all data anytime via the Application's settings or Android system settings.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party Services

To keep the Application stable, fix crashes, and support ongoing independent development, we integrate trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage statistics (such as screen navigation counts, feature usage frequency, and retention metrics) to help us understand how the Application is used and improve future features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous technical diagnostics and stack traces when an unexpected error or crash occurs, allowing us to publish bug fixes quickly.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Retrieves dynamic Application settings (such as feature availability flags) without requiring a full app update.
  • Google AdMob (Advertising): When ad monetization is enabled, AdMob uses device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and device information to serve relevant native or interstitial advertisements. AdMob operations are strictly gated by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for full GDPR / IAB TCF v2.2 compliance in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom.
  • Google Play Billing: Handles secure in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads Forever"). All billing details and payment processing are securely handled directly by Google Play. We do not collect or store your credit card numbers or banking information.

2. Android Device Permissions

The Application requests certain device permissions to enable specific optional features:

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Required only if you choose to record voice memos inside your journal entries. Audio files remain on your device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to deliver daily journal reminder notifications scheduled by you.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to reschedule your daily journal alarms following a device reboot.
  • VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during interactive canvas features.

3. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance (EEA & UK Users)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK), you possess specific rights regarding your personal data under the GDPR:

  • Explicit Consent & Control: Before serving personalized advertisements, our Application presents a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form adhering to the IAB TCF v2.2 framework.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may change or withdraw your ad consent selection at any time by navigating to Settings > Privacy & Compliance > Ad Privacy Choices inside the Application.
  • Data Erasure: Since personal content resides on your local device, deleting the Application or clearing its storage instantly erases all local journal data.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal information. Third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect unique device identifiers for advertising purposes subject to your device settings. You may opt out of interest-based ads via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID).

5. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.
